## Acceptance gate

Manual-review statuses: APPROVED, APPROVED_WITH_RESTRICTIONS, INSUFFICIENT_RESPONSE, REJECTED.

Campaign #4 may proceed only when the written response clearly establishes private storage, required transformations, private backtesting, private quantitative research, private ML training, and derived-model creation/use. Future commercial licensing is tracked separately; if private research is explicit but commercial use is not, record `APPROVED_WITH_RESTRICTIONS` for research and keep commercial deployment blocked.
