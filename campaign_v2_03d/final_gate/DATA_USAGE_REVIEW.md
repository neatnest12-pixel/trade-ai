# HistData Usage Review`n`nOfficial HistData materials identify downloadable EUR/USD historical files and bid-based M1 data. The available materials do not clearly grant all requested rights for local private storage, automated transformation, private ML/backtesting, and derived models. Redistribution/resale restrictions are not permission for research, but the private-use rights remain insufficiently explicit. Usage gate: MANUAL_CONFIRMATION_REQUIRED.
