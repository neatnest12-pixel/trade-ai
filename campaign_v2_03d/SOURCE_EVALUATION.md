# Source Evaluation

TrueFX is the selected candidate: institutional top-of-book tick data sourced from Integral OCX, with EURUSD historical downloads and stated internal-use licensing. TrueFX explicitly requires free registration/login before historical downloads; no credentials are available in this environment. Dukascopy also exposes EURUSD historical tick/M1 data, but its usage terms and download authorization require separate acceptance/verification. No source was silently ingested.
